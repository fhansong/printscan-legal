Thanks for using Homework Print. We support learning templates, printing homework files and email attachments, scanning worksheets, and printing with your iPhone or iPad. The app is designed for parents and teachers and works locally on your device.

Before You Contact Us

  • Make sure your iPhone/iPad and printer are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  • Allow Local Network access when iOS asks. Without it, the App cannot discover printers.
  • If you use the Wi-Fi name display, Location permission is optional; printing still works if you deny it.
  • Try the built-in test page or a simple PDF first.

The App cannot find my printer

Check that the printer is powered on and connected to the same network, that AirPrint/IPP is enabled on the printer, and that Local Network permission is allowed for Homework Print in iOS Settings. Some networks block device-to-device discovery; try a different Wi-Fi network or a mobile hotspot.

Printing starts but the page is blank or cut off

Confirm the paper size and orientation in the print panel. Very wide web pages or fixed-width tables may need to be printed from a PDF instead. You can also try the system print panel’s preview before sending the job.

My network scanner is not found

The App uses eSCL/AirScan-compatible scanning. Make sure the scanner is on the same network and has network scanning enabled. If your scanner does not support eSCL, use the camera scanning flow instead.

How do I print an email?

Open the Email entry in the App and choose Choose .eml File, then select an .eml file exported from your mail app. Alternatively, open an email attachment in your mail app, tap Share, and choose Homework Print. Email content is parsed locally and is not uploaded to us.

Where are my files stored?

Imported files, scans and logs are stored locally on your device. You can delete them in the App or remove them by uninstalling the App. We cannot access or recover files from your device.
