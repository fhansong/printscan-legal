Privacy Policy

App: Print & Scan
Developer: fzx
Effective date: November 20, 2026
Contact: fzxfzx5539@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Print & Scan (“the App”) handles information. The App is designed to work locally on your device. We do not operate a server that stores your documents, photos, emails, or scans.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require an account. We do not collect, upload, sell, or share your documents, photos, email content, printer names, scan content, or contact list. We do not use advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

2. Files, Printing, and Scanning

Files you import are processed on your device and sent directly to the printer or scanner you select. Print jobs and scan jobs are transmitted to devices on your local network. We do not receive copies of those jobs.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan a document or photo.
  • Photos: used only when you choose photos to print or edit.
  • Local Network: used to discover and communicate with printers and scanners on your Wi-Fi network.
  • Location (optional): used only to display the current Wi-Fi network name. It is not used for advertising or tracking. You can deny it and still print.

4. Email and Web Content

Email attachments and .eml files are parsed locally on your device. If you use the web printing feature, the App loads the website you choose in a WebView. That website may collect information under its own privacy policy. If an email contains remote images, your device may contact the image host; you can choose not to load remote images.

5. Logs and Diagnostics

Print and scan logs are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to us. You can clear them in the App or by deleting the App.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Imported files, scans, templates, and logs remain on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App. Because we do not store this data on a server, we cannot access or delete it remotely.

7. Third-Party Services

The App uses Apple system services, such as StoreKit for review prompts and the system print panel. These services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy. We do not integrate advertising or analytics SDKs.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International Users

The App is designed to process data locally on your device. If you use third-party websites through the web printing feature, those websites may process data in other countries under their own policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will be updated. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact
